UPDATE 1.30 CHANGES THE LANDSCAPE.

Alongside the expansion, Update 1.30 brings new ways to travel, clearer inventory visuals and changes to wildlife and infected encounters.

  • Motorbikes: mopeds and enduro bikes, with new handling physics and crash reactions.
  • Inventory: more than 150 icons updated for consistency and legibility.
  • Infected: attacks better reflect survivor height and position, with an additional running heavy attack.

01 // THE EXPERIENCE

WELCOME TO CHERNARUS.

DayZ drops you into a post-apocalyptic world with no scripted path to safety. Explore abandoned settlements, manage your health and resources, and decide who you can trust.

The environment keeps score. Hunger, thirst, illness, weather and exhaustion can change a journey in seconds. The result is a survival experience where getting through the next hour can be an achievement.

Bandages stop bleeding and protect wounds. Disinfect them when possible to reduce the risk of wound infection.

Epinephrine can wake an unconscious survivor and briefly boost stamina. Morphine can temporarily ease movement with a fracture, but does not heal it.

Multivitamins temporarily support immunity. Keep your treatments together in a first aid pouch, which stores supplies but does not treat injuries itself.

WHY I LOVE DAYZ.

I enjoy DayZ because every trip into the wilderness can turn into its own story. Exploring, scavenging, managing the risks, and never knowing who you might meet make each session feel different.
